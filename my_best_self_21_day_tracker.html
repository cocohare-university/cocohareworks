How it works

  1. Pick three well-being strategies to practice.
  2. Write a concrete plan for each strategy.
  3. Track your minutes and reflections each day.
  4. At the end of each week, consider adjustments.
  5. After 21 days, review what worked and what to continue.
The original workbook's three strategy sheets are represented here, including plans, daily minutes, notes, weekly revisions, and final summaries.
